# Perspectives: Vadym & Lisa

> Last updated: 2026-03-29
> This file captures each person's individual stance, concerns, and priorities so the agent can provide balanced advice that respects both perspectives.

---

## Lisa's Perspective

### Core Belief
Fix the best rate available *now*, and renegotiate downward if rates drop. Act quickly; don't wait for a better deal that may never come.

### Strategy
1. Find the lowest available rate on the market using aggregators.
2. Lock it in as soon as possible (before the rate review / potential rate rises).
3. If rates fall, go back and update the deal — repeat as often as needed to stay on the best available rate.

### Primary Fear
> **Missing the moment when rates are at their lowest, only for them to rise afterwards.**

She is most concerned about the downside of inaction — that hesitating will leave them stuck when rates move upward.

### Approach to Decision-Making
- Pragmatic and action-oriented.
- Prefers to contact lenders directly (via written communication rather than phone).
- Open to using aggregators without necessarily going through a broker.

### Current Concerns
- Short-term concerns: focused on the rate decision.
- No major long-term concerns beyond getting the rate right.

## Vadym's Perspective

### Core Belief
Be careful not to lock in prematurely at a suboptimal rate — especially for a long term (5 years). Rates may fall meaningfully in 2–3 years, and a premature long fix could be costly.

### Strategy
- Agrees with the urgency to start looking now.
- Wants the process to be actively managed (not just fixed and forgotten).
- Prefers to keep options open through shorter fix terms or products with low ERCs.
- Wants the full context documented so future remortgage decisions can benefit from this groundwork.

### Concerns

1. **Premature lock-in**: Signing up for a rate now (especially 5-year fixed) that turns out to be worse than what the market offers in 2–3 years.
   > *"If we do it for 5 years now, the difference in rates 5.2 isn't big and I have an expectation that in 2-3 years the situation might improve a lot."*

2. **Passive management**: Fixing a rate, then not actively monitoring whether better deals have emerged.
   > *"I have a worry that if we find some rate and then we don't do it, meaning we won't actively deal with it, then we will just reserve it and won't deal with it."*

3. **Extra work from switching**: If they fix now and rates drop, they'll need to re-engage the process — he sees this as likely but necessary to manage.

### Aspirations for the AI Agent
- Provide a living knowledge base that persists across remortgage cycles (not just this one).
- Give advice that is **contextually grounded** in their actual financial situation (income, LTV, savings, life plans) — not generic market commentary.
- Balance both Vadym's and Lisa's viewpoints fairly.
- Help work through the validity of each concern objectively, as a neutral advisor.
