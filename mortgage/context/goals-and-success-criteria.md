# Goals & Success Criteria

> Last updated: 2026-03-29

---

## Primary Goal

**Lock in the most financially advantageous mortgage rate possible before the current fix expires in October 2026, without missing a better opportunity if rates fall.**

---

## What "Success" Looks Like

Success is evaluated over a 5-year horizon. The deal will be considered successful if:

1. We did **not miss the moment** when the lowest available rate was offered during the remortgage window.
2. We were **actively monitoring** rate changes and responded to any meaningful drops by renegotiating or switching to a new product.
3. We did not lock ourselves into a rate that was significantly higher than what the market offered 6–24 months later (particularly relevant for 5-year fixes).
4. The monthly payment remained **manageable on a single income** (in the event of parental leave or reduced hours).

> Lisa's formulation: *"We didn't miss the moment when we had the opportunity to fix a deal at a lower interest."*

---

## Sub-Goals

- **Start searching immediately** — the 6-month window has just opened; early action preserves maximum optionality.
- **Maintain flexibility** — avoid over-committing to a long fixed period (e.g. 5 years) if market consensus points to rate reductions ahead.
- **Stay informed** — receive timely alerts on Bank of England decisions, lender product changes, and market rate shifts.
- **Make joint decisions** — both Vadym and Lisa's perspectives must be represented in any agent analysis or recommendation (see `perspectives.md`).

---

## Key Risk to Avoid

The primary risk both Vadym and Lisa agree on:

> **Rates rise, and we failed to fix before they did.**

Secondary risk (Vadym's concern):

> **We lock in for too long (e.g. 5 years) at today's rates, and miss a significantly better market in 2–3 years.**

---

## Decision Criteria for Choosing a Deal

When evaluating mortgage products, the agent should weigh the following:

| Criterion | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Interest rate** | Lower is better; compare 2-year vs 5-year fixed |
| **Fee structure** | Product fees can offset headline rate savings on smaller balances — always compare Total Cost |
| **Flexibility / ERC** | Early Repayment Charges matter if we want to switch again if rates drop |
| **Fix term** | 2-year offers more flexibility; 5-year locks in for longer |
| **Monthly payment** | Must be survivable on one income (~£5,700–£5,833/month net) |
| **LTV bracket** | Currently ~85% LTV — confirm if crossing a lower LTV threshold (e.g. 80%) unlocks meaningfully better rates |

---

## North Star Principle

> Monitor actively, fix decisively, stay flexible. This is not a set-and-forget decision.
